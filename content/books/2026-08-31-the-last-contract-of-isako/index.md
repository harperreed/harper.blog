In "The Last Contract of Isako," author Fonda Lee weaves a captivating standalone tale that plunges readers into a gripping dystopian landscape. Isako, portrayed as a formidable swordswoman and corporate samurai, stands at a crossroads following the unexpected retirement of her long-time client. Intent on embracing her death gracefully in the unforgiving cold of their barren world, Isako is unexpectedly pulled back into action with an irresistible final mission.

The intricately crafted narrative reveals Isako's fraught relationship with her last and most detestable apprentice, Martim, who has seized a position of power that brings danger. This mission catapults Isako into a maelstrom of corporate machinations, espionage, and dark secrets where every decision could lead to either salvation or ruin. Lee's storytelling brilliance shines through, capturing the essence of loyalty being sacrificed at the altar of wealth and ambition.
