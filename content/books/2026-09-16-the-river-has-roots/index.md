In "The River Has Roots," Amal El-Mohtar takes readers on a lyrical journey into the mystical town of Thistleford, where the mundane and the magical intertwine. Here, two sisters, Esther and Ysabel, are bound by an unbreakable love and a shared destiny tied to the enchanted willows that grow near their home. These trees are not merely plants but powerful beings that thrive on the songs sung to them by the Hawthorn family, keeping an ancient pact that has endured through generations. 

As Esther wrestles with her heart, she rejects a persistent suitor in favor of a romance that blossomed in the breathtaking, dangerous land of Faerie. This choice threatens not only her sisterhood with Ysabel but also their very lives, as the forces of the magical realm loom closer, ready to challenge their bond. 

Amal El-Mohtar, acclaimed for her compelling storytelling and imaginative worlds, crafts a tale that explores themes of love, sacrifice, and the complexities of familial ties. With an average rating of 3.94 from over 52,000 ratings, "The River Has Roots" invites readers into a world of enchanting prose where every decision hangs between devotion and peril.
