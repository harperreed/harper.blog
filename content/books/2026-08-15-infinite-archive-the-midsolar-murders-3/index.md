In 'Infinite Archive,' part of the Midsolar Murders series, we step into the life of Mallory Viridian, an amateur sleuth who has become accustomed to the dramatic flair of crime on Station Eternity. After a period of relative calm without any murders—a dramatic break from her often turbulent reality—Mallory finds herself growing restless. Just as she settles into a routine, everything changes with the imminent arrival of the 'Metis,' a revolutionary data ship bringing the entire Internet from Earth and an exciting mystery fan convention to her corner of the galaxy.

As anticipation builds, Mallory is unexpectedly assigned as the keynote speaker at the convention, a role she has mixed feelings about. Her literary agent arrives with the ship, and she hopes his presence will reignite her stagnant career. However, in a twist of fate, tragedy strikes when she discovers her agent dead. With a new and enigmatic alien operating in the shadows, and the specter of a powerful and unknown killer lurking at the convention, Mallory is swiftly drawn into a web of intrigue that challenges not just her instincts but her very survival.
