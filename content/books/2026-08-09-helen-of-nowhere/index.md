From Makenna Goodman, acclaimed author of 'The Shame', comes a captivating exploration of one man’s turbulent quest for renewal in 'Helen of Nowhere'. This story unfolds over the course of a single day, navigating through the intricate layers of an unnamed protagonist’s fractured existence right at the turning point of his career and marriage. 

Visiting an idyllic countryside home, he is introduced to its ethereal former owner, Helen, whose aura pervades the very timbers of the house. As the realtor shares fragments of Helen’s compelling life, the man confronts the poignant contrast between her vibrant past and his stagnant present. It is a narrative woven with philosophical inquiry and emotional depth, pressing readers to confront the profound question: What is the true cost of happiness? 

Characterized as 'brilliantly complex' by Jenny Offill, this novel invites readers not only into a world of real estate and dreams but into an intimate examination of selfhood and the stories that mold us. With an average rating of 3.22 from over 1,569 readers, Goodman's thoughtful prose also resonates with themes of longing, regrets, and the search for meaning. 'Helen of Nowhere' is not just about a house; it's a mirror reflecting our desires and choices, demanding that we exploring what it means to truly live.
