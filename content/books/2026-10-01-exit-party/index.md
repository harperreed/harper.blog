In *Exit Party*, Emily St. John Mandel, the acclaimed author of *Station Eleven* and *Sea of Tranquility*, invites readers to 2031 Los Angeles, a shimmering but unstable city recovering from civil collapse. The once-vibrant cityscape is now characterized by peacekeeping troops and makeshift living arrangements amid illuminated Jacarandas and an uplifted curfew. 

As the protagonist Ari Waker navigates a lively party, a startling incident unfolds: the host, Kareem, vanishes, replaced momentarily by a bewildered twin—a doppelganger that foreshadows deeper currents of identity and loss. How does one piece together the mystery of a sudden disappearance when the very world is unraveling at its core? Ari—a gunrunner and art collector—embarks on a perilous journey through both physical and metaphysical realms, from the haunted streets of Los Angeles to opulent resorts in Greece, and ultimately to the vastness of colonies on the moon.

Mandel's narrative weaves an intricate tapestry of double identities, questions of surveillance, the fragility of safety, and the dark truths that linger behind resonant social commentary. The author demonstrates her literary prowess by constructing a gripping layer of suspense on a backdrop of dystopian elegance. Embracing themes of resilience and how humanity forges connections even in dire circumstances, *Exit Party* promises to leave a lasting imprint on its readers.
